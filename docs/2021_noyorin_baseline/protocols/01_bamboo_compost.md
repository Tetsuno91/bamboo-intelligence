# Protocol 01: 100% Bamboo-Derived Static Compost (Zero-Turning, Zero-Added-Water, Indoor)
# プロトコル01：原料100％竹堆肥（初期攪拌のみ・無切り返し・無加水・屋内管理方式）

## Metadata / 基本情報
- **Protocol ID:** `BI-P01-BAMBOO-COMPOST`
- **Location / 実施場所:** Minamiizu, Shizuoka, Japan（静岡県南伊豆町・入宇津農作業所）
- **Baseline Reference / 基準資料:** 2021 Agro-Forestry Presentation (`2021_noyorin_slides.pdf`)
- **Last Updated / 最終更新:** 2026-11
- **Inputs Policy / 資材方針:** 100% bamboo-derived biomass; zero livestock manure, zero synthetic fertilizers, zero chemical herbicides（竹由来原料100％・家畜糞尿および化学資材不使用）

---

## 1. Material Composition & Ratios / 原料構成と配合比

All solid ratios are measured by **volume using loader bucket units** (skid-steer loader).
固形原料の配合はすべて**ローダーのバケット杯数による容積比**で管理する。

| Material / 原料 | Volume Ratio / 容積比 | Unit / 単位 | Notes / 備考 |
| :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Bamboo chips / 竹チップ** | **10** | Bucket / バケット | Chipped on-site in bamboo stands via self-propelled chipper |
| **Porous bamboo charcoal / ポーラス竹炭** | **1** | Bucket / バケット | High-temperature, soft, porous on-site carbonized charcoal |
| **Bamboo shoot husks / タケノコの皮** | **2** | Bucket / バケット | Harvested seasonal byproduct |

### Liquid Additives & Moisture Control / 液体資材と水分管理
- **Added Water / 加水:** **None (0)** — No external water is added; fermentation relies solely on the inherent moisture of the bamboo biomass and shoot husks.（外部からの加水は一切行わない。原料本来の水分のみで発酵させる）
- **Bamboo Vinegar / 竹酢液:** **500x dilution**, applied **once during initial mixing only**.（現在は500倍希釈液を使用。初期攪拌時に1回のみ散布）

---

## 2. Process Parameters & Environment / 製造工程・環境条件

1. **Initial Mixing (初期攪拌):** Mix bamboo chips (10), porous bamboo charcoal (1), and bamboo shoot husks (2) thoroughly by loader bucket while applying 500x diluted bamboo vinegar once.
2. **Turning Frequency (切り返し回数):** **0 times after initial mixing.** No subsequent mechanical turning is performed.（初期攪拌のみで、熟成期間中の切り返しは行わない）
3. **Maturation Period (発酵熟成期間):** **120 days** static aerobic fermentation.（120日間の静置発酵熟成）
4. **Curing Environment (堆積環境):** **Indoor stacking** inside the covered compost barn.（屋根のある屋内での積み上げ管理）

---

## 3. Process Evolution: Outdoor to Indoor Transition / 工程の改善履歴（露天から屋内へ）

- **Previous Method (2021 Baseline / 過去の方式):** Open-air (outdoor) stacking exposed to rainfall.（露天での積み上げ）
- **Field Observation (現場観察):** During rain events, liquid leachate ("chip juice") seeped out from the pile into the surrounding ground. Weeds growing around the runoff perimeter exhibited markedly vigorous growth compared to surrounding areas.（降雨のたびにチップの汁が滲み出し、周囲の雑草の伸びが非常に良かった）
- **Engineering Decision (改善の判断):** The vigorous weed growth served as a direct bio-indicator of nutrient and soluble extract leaching. To prevent active components from washing away and to retain full fertility inside the compost, the process was transitioned to **indoor stacking**.（成分の流出・溶脱を防ぎ堆肥内に留めるため、屋内での積み上げ方式へ移行した）

---

## 4. Control Conditions & Experimental Boundaries / 対照実験・境界条件

- **Trial without Porous Bamboo Charcoal (ポーラス竹炭なし条件):** **N = 0 (Untested / 未実施)**
  - Porous bamboo charcoal is treated as a mandatory baseline component rather than an optional additive. No comparative failure trials omitting charcoal have been conducted.
  - 本手法ではポーラス竹炭を必須の基本構成要素として位置づけているため、炭を抜いた比較試験は想定・実施していない。
  - *Open Research Variable:* Whether zero-turning, zero-water static fermentation succeeds or turns anaerobic without the 1-part porous charcoal ratio remains an untested control variable for future external validation.

---

## 5. Field Outcomes & Mechanistic Notes / 現場成果と構造メモ
- **Observed Outcome (2021 Baseline):** Steam generation during fermentation and stable maturation into dark, humus-rich compost used for citrus orchard establishment (transplanted May 2019, confirmed established Oct 2021).
- **Structural Note:** Achieving stable 120-day indoor maturation without turning or watering—while retaining soluble leachates previously lost to rain—indicates that the `10:1:2` volumetric matrix naturally balances C/N activation (shoot husks), moisture retention, and passive aeration/microbial buffering (porous bamboo charcoal).
