# Agro-Forestry Baseline Record (2021): Utilization of Unused Bamboo Resources in Minamiizu
# 「農用林」としての取組（2021年ベースライン記録）― 未利用資源である竹の活用 ―

## Metadata / 基本情報
- **Location / 対象地域:** Southern Izu Peninsula (Kamo / Minamiizu area), Shizuoka, Japan
- **Geographic Context / 地理特性:** Southernmost tip of Izu Peninsula directly facing the Kuroshio Current; tightly coupled mountain-to-ocean ecosystem ("山と海はひとつながり")
- **Baseline Year / 基準年:** 2021
- **Source Slides / 元スライド:** [`2021_noyorin_slides.pdf`](./2021_noyorin_slides.pdf)
- **Core Concept / 核となる循環:** Human-centered circulation connecting Bamboo, Soil, Agriculture, Food, and Culture（人を中心に据えた「竹→土→農→食→文化」の循環）

---

## 2026 Retrospective Update: Critical Sequencing Constraint / 2026年現在地点からの重要追記（施工順序の制約）

> **Key Field Finding / 現場での気づき:**
> **Vertical bamboo charcoal shaft installation MUST precede the application of bamboo compost or spent shiitake logs.**
> **「縦穴竹炭打設」が先、「竹堆肥・廃ほだ木」の投入が後。この正しい順序を踏んでこそ、現場の労力は実りとなって返ってくる。**

- **Original Motivation / 当初の動機:** The 2021 organic-input approaches (bamboo compost and spent shiitake logs) were initiated to revive Moso bamboo stands degraded by past chemical fertilizer applications（化学肥料の投与によって衰退した竹林を蘇らせたいという思いからスタートした取組である）。
- **Limitation of Reverse-Order Intervention / 逆順アプローチの限界:** While partial positive effects were confirmed (e.g., localized humus formation and winter bamboo shoot emergence), applying organic matter without first resolving the underlying soil degradation cannot deliver the expected full-scale recovery（一部で良い効果は確認できたものの、土壌の根本解決ができていない状態では思うような効果は期待できず、逆順からのアプローチでは効果的とは言えない面がある）。
- **Recommended Sequence (as of 2026) / 2026年現在の推奨シーケンス:**
  1. **Step 1 — Root-Cause Soil Restoration / 根本解決（地下環境の基盤整備）:** Install vertical porous bamboo charcoal shafts first to restore subsurface aeration, drainage, and oxidation-reduction balance.
  2. **Step 2 — Organic & Fungal Amendment / 有機物・菌類資材の投入:** Apply 100% bamboo compost (`Protocol 01`) or spent shiitake logs (`Protocol 02`) onto the restored soil foundation so that field labor translates directly into durable ecological and harvest returns.

## Key Field Observations & Practices (2021 Baseline) / 主な現場実践と観察記録

### 1. 100% Bamboo-Derived Compost / 原料100％竹堆肥
- **Detailed Protocol / 詳細プロトコル:** [`01_bamboo_compost.md`](./protocols/01_bamboo_compost.md)
- **Ingredients / 原料:** Bamboo chips (10), porous bamboo charcoal (1), bamboo shoot husks (2), 500x bamboo vinegar（竹チップ10：ポーラス竹炭1：タケノコの皮2＋竹酢液500倍希釈）
- **Process / 工程:** 120-day static indoor fermentation without turning or added water（初期攪拌のみ・無切り返し・無加水・屋内120日発酵）

### 2. Thermal Conversion & Microbial Dynamics / 炭化技術と菌類の働き
- **Porous Bamboo Charcoal / ポーラス竹炭:** High-temperature, simple on-site carbonization yielding soft, porous charcoal suited for soil amendment and beneficial microbial habitats.
- **Bamboo Vinegar / 竹酢液:** Collected from smoke produced during traditional Izu charcoal kiln firing and refining.
- **Fungal & Actinomycete Activity / 菌床化:** Spread bamboo chips naturally act as fungal beds, generating mushrooms and dense white actinomycetes.

### 3. Ecological Constraint: Deer Grazing / 生態系の制約条件：鹿の食害
- **Counter-intuitive Finding / 現場の現実:** Contrary to the common assumption that abandoned bamboo forests expand endlessly, heavy deer browsing on emerging bamboo shoots causes visible stand decline.

### 4. Cross-Resource Synergy: Spent Shiitake Logs × Bamboo Shoots / シイタケ廃ほだ木×竹林育成
- **Detailed Protocol / 詳細プロトコル:** [`02_shiitake_logs_to_bamboo.md`](./protocols/02_shiitake_logs_to_bamboo.md)
- **Method / 手法:** Transporting spent shiitake mushroom logs (7 loads of a 2-ton dump truck) into bamboo stands, leveraging shiitake mycelium to break down woody biomass into rich forest floor humus.
- **Observed Result / 実証結果:** Accelerated fine root development from bamboo rhizomes and winter bamboo shoot harvesting ("Christmas bamboo shoot digging") starting from Year 2 post-application.

### 5. Timber Utilization & Coppicing of *Sequoia sempervirens* / センペルセコイアの活用と萌芽更新
- **Utilization / 用材利用:** Harvesting oversized windbreak *Sequoia sempervirens*, milling into boards, smoke-drying in a kiln, and applying water-resistant lumber to the exterior walls of the compost barn.
- **Coppicing Measurement / 萌芽実測:** Stumps felled in **July 2013** regenerated via coppicing to a measured height of **5 meters by February 2021** (~7.5 years).

### 6. Agricultural Trial: Citrus Cultivation / 柑橘園地での竹堆肥試験
- **Baseline Planting / 定植:** **May 26, 2019** — Transplanted citrus saplings with matured bamboo compost onto newly cleared land.
- **Follow-up Observation / 経過確認:** **October 20, 2021** — Confirmed establishment, vegetative growth, and fruiting, linking safe agricultural production back to forest management and ocean health.
